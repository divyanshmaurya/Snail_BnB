Our story

Built by snails, for snails

Snail BnB started in 2017 when our founder, Lady Escargot, noticed travelling snails sleeping under any old leaf. She knew gastropods deserved better. Four years later (we work at our own pace), the platform launched.

The slow travel manifesto

Modern travel is obsessed with speed. Get there faster, see more, do more. We reject all of that — partly on principle, mostly out of biological necessity.

At Snail BnB we believe the journey is the holiday. When it takes a season to reach your destination, you notice things: every dew drop, every promising patch of lichen, every close call with a thrush. And when you finally arrive at a shell-ter that's damp, shaded and structurally delicious, you appreciate it properly.

We are proudly slug-inclusive. Shells are a lifestyle, not a requirement.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

Work backwards from your travel speed. A stay two gardens away means leaving roughly a season before check-in, so we recommend booking at least two seasons ahead. The Damp Log Lodge books out a full year in advance.

What are "leaves" and how do I pay?

Leaves are our platform currency. One leaf is one standard-sized unit of appetising foliage. We accept romaine, hosta, and (at a premium exchange rate) basil. Dandelion is legal tender but frowned upon.

Are slugs welcome?

Absolutely. Snail BnB has been slug-inclusive since day one. Every property offers shell-optional accommodation, and our mucus spas cater to all body types.

What if it rains during my stay?

Then congratulations — you've received a complimentary upgrade. Rain is our five-star weather tier and no extra leaves are charged.

What's the cancellation policy?

Very flexible. Given average travel times, if you cancel and turn around immediately, you'll be home before the host notices. Full leaf refunds up to one season before check-in.

Is there a loyalty programme?

Yes — TrailPoints™. Earn one point per night and redeem them for spa credits, salad-bar upgrades, or our exclusive "I went somewhere" shell sticker.
